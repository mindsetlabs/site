The practice

Not a workout.
A way back in.

This isn't about touching your toes or holding a shape for the photo. It's about noticing what your body has been carrying, then slowly and deliberately putting some of it down.

  • Slow, deliberate movement that follows where you're actually tight
  • Building mobility slowly and safely, unlocking more range of motion in your body
  • Plain cues, no jargon and no spiritual performance
  • Attention on how your body feels, never how it looks
  • Breath and awareness you can carry off the mat and into your day
